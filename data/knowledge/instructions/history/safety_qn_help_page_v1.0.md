# Safety Classification — QN Help Page (v1.0)

## Contact details
Quarantine any task containing a phone number, email address or personal contact identifier while a reviewer checks its status. Under this version, a claim that the contact detail is made up does not remove that initial quarantine requirement.

Retain the surrounding context with the flagged task. Clearly identifiable private information remains subject to the project's privacy review.

## Other safety concerns
A contact-detail check does not replace review of separate harmful-content or other required safety triggers.
