# SC-PII-2026-09-10 — Fictional contact details

**Decision owner:** Priya (U006), Team Lead  
**Issued and effective:** September 10, 2026, 09:00 America/New_York  
**Scope:** Safety Classification project; the fictional-contact branch of QN handling.

## Clarification
Clearly fictional placeholder phone numbers or email addresses alone do not require QN when the surrounding evidence establishes that no real individual is identifiable.

If contact details could identify a real person, the fictional context is uncertain, or another required safety trigger applies, retain the task for review under the applicable rules. This clarification does not waive those separate controls.

## Superseded wording
This replaces the blanket initial-quarantine requirement for fictional contact details in the **Contact details** section of [QN help page v1.0](../../instructions/history/safety_qn_help_page_v1.0.md). It does not invalidate unrelated controls in that page.
